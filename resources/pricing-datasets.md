# Pricing Data

Prices change every few weeks. Never type a price into a cost sheet without its source and the date you checked it.

**[Pydantic genai-prices](https://github.com/pydantic/genai-prices)** · *Job: keep the prices in your cost model current.*
Machine-readable prices for the main providers, including cached and tiered pricing, updated continuously.
Why trust it: maintained by the Pydantic team, MIT licence, updated almost daily.

**[llm-prices.com](https://www.llm-prices.com/)** (Simon Willison) · *Job: price one prompt across models in a minute.*
Type your input and output size, see the cost on every major model side by side.
Why trust it: kept by one of the most followed independent voices on LLMs; the data is [open on GitHub](https://github.com/simonw/llm-prices).

**[Artificial Analysis](https://artificialanalysis.ai/)** · *Job: compare price against quality and speed, not price alone.*
Independent benchmarks that put each model's price next to its quality score and speed.
Why trust it: independent and widely cited. Not open source, but free to read.

## Before you use any price, write down

- Source link and the date you checked it
- Model version
- Input price and output price
- Cached-input rules
- Batch discount
- Price changes above a context size
- Region or account caveats
